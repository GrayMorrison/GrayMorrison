Artist biography
Gray Morrison is a multi-instrumentalist, singer, and songwriter from Northern Ireland whose music blends raw intensity with resonant melody and fearless honesty. His sound is driven by soaring guitars steeped in the spirit of better decades, relentless rhythms, and lyrics that cut to the core of what it means to be human. Through songs that explore love and loss, memory and survival, Morrison confronts the human condition while searching for meaning in the mysteries that shape our world. Drawing on influences from rock, post-punk, folk, jazz, blues, country, and electronic music, Morrison crafts songs that are both fierce and fragile, unafraid to face the darkness yet always reaching toward the light. The name Gray Morrison is a nom de plume, drawn from the artist’s university nickname and his mother’s maiden name.

Debut EP (self-titled)
Recorded between March and December 2025, this collection was born from a time of upheaval, capturing the fragile balance between darkness and hope. Each track reaches toward meaning, connection, and the possibility of redemption, or the quiet acceptance of closure. Morrison is joined by Joanne Rowland on vocals, longtime school friend Rosie Marc (Jayne Harkness, formerly of Vokxen) on drums, David Jamison (formerly of Clay) on drums and percussion, and a guest performance from Michael Sidwell (of Leithinis) on keyboards. Recorded, mixed, and mastered by David Jamison at Millstone Studios, County Down, Northern Ireland. The album was released on 30/1/2026. Crepuscular Rays
